HEXBOARD SEQUENCER SCREEN AND OVERLAY REQUIREMENTS

ID: SEQ-UI-001
Title: Edit Overlay
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
Selecting a step shall show an edit overlay for that step.

Details:
- The header shall use the form Edit #NN.
- The overlay shall show a compact info line in the form L ...% V ... P ...%.
- The overlay shall show the selected step notes in ascending order across one
  or two OLED note lines.
- Steps whose note labels fit on one line shall not reserve a blank second line.
- Step note labels shall follow the current tuning naming style.
- If the selected step has Tie enabled, the note area shall show `T` instead of
  note labels.


ID: SEQ-UI-002
Title: Selected Step Blink
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
The selected step shall blink to show selection.

Details:
- Blink timing is 0.6 seconds on and 0.2 seconds off.
- Empty selected steps blink visibly instead of staying dark.
- A selected empty step uses the highest step-light level while blinking.


ID: SEQ-UI-003
Title: Selected Step Brightness
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
During the visible phase of the selected-step blink, a note step shall use a
brighter selected-note color that preserves the note's hue better than the play
highlight tint.

Details:
- Selected note steps shall use the high level in the sequencer step-light
  brightness ladder.
- An accented selected note step shall keep the same hue and use that high
  brightness level.


ID: SEQ-UI-004
Title: Step Tools Overlay
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
The step tools overlay shall show the selected step number, the compact length
length, velocity, and probability line, the selected step notes across one or
two OLED note lines, and the
currently available tool labels.

Details:
- Step note labels shall follow the current tuning naming style.
- Steps whose note labels fit on one line shall not reserve a blank second line.
- If the selected step has Tie enabled, the note area shall show `T` instead of
  note labels.
- While this overlay is open, the normal sequencer step LEDs shall remain
  visible.
- The tool buttons shall move to lower rows so the top 4 step rows remain free
  for step selection.
- Tool selection keys shall be lit blue at the sequencer medium light level.


ID: SEQ-UI-005
Title: Exact Length Overlay
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
The exact length overlay shall show the selected step number, the current value,
a separate new-entry value with a blinking underscore cursor, the keypad
layout, and a save instruction.

Details:
- The overlay header shall use the form `Exact Length #NN`.
- The current-value row and new-entry row shall be vertically compact with no
  blank spacer line between them.
- The top 4 sequencer step rows shall remain visible while the exact-length
  keypad is open.
- The exact-length keypad shall move to the lower board area.
- Exact-length keypad keys shall be lit blue at the sequencer medium light
  level.
- Backspace is on the eighth-row first key.
- Cancel uses the same lower cancel area as the tools picker.


ID: SEQ-UI-006
Title: Exact Velocity Overlay
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
The exact velocity overlay shall show the selected step number, the compact
length, velocity, and probability line, the selected step notes across one or
two OLED note lines, the large current velocity value, and the short encoder
instructions.

Details:
- Exact velocity editing is encoder-driven and does not require dedicated lit
  button hints.
- While this overlay is open, the normal sequencer step LEDs and function LEDs
  shall remain visible.
- While this overlay is open, tapping another step pad shall switch the
  velocity editor to that step without saving pending changes from the previous
  step.
- While this overlay is open, lower note-entry keys shall not edit notes.
- The helper text shall instruct the user to press the encoder to save.
- Steps whose note labels fit on one line shall not reserve a blank second line.
- If the selected step has Tie enabled, the note area shall show `T` instead of
  note labels.

ID: SEQ-UI-007
Title: Overview Overlay
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
The overview overlay shall pack in as many steps as fit on screen and page
through the 32-step pattern.

Details:
- Empty steps show underscores.
- Note labels follow the current tuning naming style.
- Steps that fit on one line shall not reserve a blank second line.
- Steps may use a second indented line when needed so all stored notes remain
  visible.
- A tied step shall show `T` by itself with no appended note labels.


ID: SEQ-UI-008
Title: Naming Overlay
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
Naming mode shall show the current name line, a blinking cursor, and the
on-screen character layout.

Details:
- Naming input keys shall be lit blue at the sequencer medium light level.
- Non-input keys shall stay dark while naming is active.


ID: SEQ-UI-009
Title: Performance Monitor Overlay
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: High

Requirement:
The performance monitor overlay shall show AudioEng, Mem, FS, MIDI Q, Drop,
and Late while the Play/Stop key is held.

Details:
- The performance monitor closes when the Play/Stop key is released.


ID: SEQ-UI-010
Title: Overlay Wake Behavior
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Active sequencer overlays shall keep the display awake while they are in use.


ID: SEQ-UI-011
Title: Status Messages
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
The sequencer shall use temporary status overlays for actions such as save,
revert, erase, and other short workflow messages.


ID: SEQ-UI-013
Title: Sequencer Now Playing Source
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
When the shared DisplayNotes setting is enabled, the board's Now Playing
overlay shall work in Sequencer mode only for manual lower-keyboard playing
when no step is selected.

Details:
- Step editing shall not drive the Now Playing overlay.
- Sequencer playback notes shall not drive the Now Playing overlay.
- The sequencer shall use the same note naming style as the keyboard overlay.


ID: SEQ-UI-014
Title: Menu Header
Status: Active
Area: UI
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Standard Sequencer menu pages shall show the current page title on one
centered, large-font header line without reducing the 11-row list.

Details:
- Menu hierarchy shall not be repeated in the header.
- The divider shall leave a small clear gap before the first menu row.
- The Sequencer performance page may use its second header line for the current
  sequence filename.
