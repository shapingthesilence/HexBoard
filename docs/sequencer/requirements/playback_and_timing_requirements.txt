HEXBOARD SEQUENCER PLAYBACK AND TIMING REQUIREMENTS

ID: SEQ-PLAY-001
Title: Step Clocking
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The sequencer shall treat each step as a 16th-note step at the current tempo.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-002
Title: Tempo Control
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide a tempo control.

Details:
- Tempo is stored in the sequence file.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-003
Title: Active Playback Length
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide a Steps control that sets the active playback
range from 1 to 32 steps.

Details:
- Step LEDs for steps beyond the active playback range shall remain off even if
  those steps still contain notes.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-004
Title: Direction Modes
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide the direction modes Forward, Backward,
Ping-Pong, Random, Brownian, and Drunk.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-005
Title: Tap Preview Control
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Playback Settings menu shall provide a Tap Preview setting with On and Off
states.

Details:
- Off means tapping a step only selects it.
- On means tapping a step selects it and previews its stored notes.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-006
Title: Play Type Control
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The Sequencer menu shall provide a Play Type setting with MIDI and OB Synth
choices.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-007
Title: Length Value Set
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Quick length editing shall use the current supported length values.

Details:
- Supported values are:
  0, 25, 50, 75, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 350, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800,
  900, 1000 percent.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-008
Title: Exact Length Entry Range
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Exact length entry shall allow direct numeric entry up to 4 digits and shall
clamp the saved value to 1000 percent maximum.

Details:
- The overlay shall show the current value and a separate new-entry field.
- Digits shall fill the new-entry field without overwriting the displayed
  current value.
- Backspace removes the previous digit.
- Cancel exits without saving changes.
- Encoder press saves the new-entry value when present, or leaves the current
  value unchanged when no new digits were entered, then returns to the tools
  picker.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-009
Title: Velocity Range
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Step velocity shall support the range 0 through 127.

Details:
- Default step velocity is 96.
- Velocity is stored per step.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-010
Title: Velocity Editing Values
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Exact velocity editing shall use coarse encoder values of 0, 5, 10, 15 and so
on up to 125, plus 127.

Details:
- Encoder press saves and returns to the tools picker.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-011
Title: Note Order
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Step notes shall be stored and displayed in ascending pitch order.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-012
Title: Chord Playback
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Playback of a step shall send all notes stored in that step together.

Details:
- Stored notes are tuning-relative pitch steps rather than raw MIDI note
  numbers.
- Playback resolves those stored pitch steps through the current Keyboard
  tuning and transpose.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-013
Title: Step Velocity Use
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The stored velocity value of a step shall affect sequencer playback, tap
preview, and live note audition while a step is selected.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-014
Title: Zero Length Behavior
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
A step with a length value of 0 percent shall not sound during playback.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-015
Title: Overlength Step Behavior
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
A step with a length value above 100 percent shall be allowed to continue
across later steps.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-015A
Title: Step Boundary Release Ordering
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
When a step reaches its scheduled note-off at a step boundary, the sequencer
shall release that note before starting the next non-tied transport step.

Details:
- A length value of exactly 100 percent shall therefore hold through its own
  step and release at the next step boundary.
- Tie is the explicit exception: when the next transport step continues that
  source step, the carried note shall remain active across the boundary
  instead of being released first.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-016
Title: Probability Range
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Step probability shall support the range 0 through 100 percent.

Details:
- Default step probability is 100 percent.
- Probability is stored per step.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-017
Title: Probability Editing Values
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Exact probability editing shall use coarse encoder values of 0, 5, 10, 15 and
so on up to 100 percent.

Details:
- Encoder press saves and returns to the tools picker.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-018
Title: Step Probability Use
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The stored probability value of a step shall control the playback chance of
that step during sequencer transport playback.

Details:
- Probability applies to the whole step rather than individual notes.
- 0 percent means the step never sounds during transport playback.
- 100 percent means the step always sounds during transport playback.
- Probability applies to both MIDI and OB Synth playback modes.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-019
Title: Tap Preview Probability Bypass
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Tap Preview and manual step preview shall ignore step probability and always
audition the step's stored notes when preview is triggered.

Details:
- Tied steps are the exception: Tap Preview and manual step preview shall stay
  silent when Tie is enabled on the selected step.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-020
Title: Tuning-Aware Playback
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
Sequencer note entry, preview, and playback shall follow the current Keyboard
tuning and transpose.

Details:
- In 12 EDO, stored notes behave like normal chromatic pitches.
- In other tunings, stored notes shall continue to use the active tuning
  mapping without requiring a different sequence file format.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-021
Title: Clock Source Behavior
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
The sequencer shall support Internal and External MIDI clock sources.

Details:
- Internal uses the local Tempo setting as the active step clock.
- External MIDI ignores the local step timer and advances playback from
  incoming MIDI clock pulses.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-022
Title: Internal MIDI Clock Send
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
When Clock Source is Internal and Send Clock is On, the sequencer shall send
MIDI clock while transport is playing.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-023
Title: MIDI Transport Send
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
When Clock Source is Internal and Send Transport is On, the sequencer shall
send MIDI Start and Stop messages when local transport starts and stops.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-024
Title: External MIDI Transport Receive
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
When Clock Source is External MIDI, incoming MIDI Start and Stop messages
shall control sequencer transport.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-025
Title: Tie Transport Behavior
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: High

Requirement:
A step with Tie enabled shall not emit a new MIDI note-on during transport
playback.

Details:
- A tied step shall continue the nearest earlier eligible non-tied step from
  the same pattern pass.
- The lower playable keyboard LEDs shall keep the continued source pitch lit
  for the full held duration while that tied playback remains active.
- The tied step's own stored note, length, velocity, and probability values
  shall be ignored during transport playback while Tie is enabled.
- If no eligible earlier source step is active when the tied step is reached,
  the tied step shall be silent.


ID: SEQ-PLAY-026
Title: Tie Loop Boundary Scope
Status: Active
Area: Playback
Priority: Medium

Requirement:
Tie v1 shall not wrap across the loop boundary.

Details:
- Step 1 shall not continue a note from the end of the pattern.
- Wraparound Tie, Slide, and other 303-style articulation extensions remain
  future work.
