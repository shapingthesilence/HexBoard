HEXBOARD SEQUENCER CONTROLS AND WORKFLOWS REQUIREMENTS

ID: SEQ-CTRL-001
Title: Step Pad Mapping
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The first 8 playable keys of the first 4 rows shall act as sequencer step pads.

Details:
- Row 1 maps to steps 1 through 8.
- Row 2 maps to steps 9 through 16.
- Row 3 maps to steps 17 through 24.
- Row 4 maps to steps 25 through 32.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-002
Title: Play Stop Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The first-row last button shall act as the Play/Stop key.

Details:
- A short press toggles transport.
- If a step is selected, a short press also deselects that step before the
  transport change happens.
- A hold of about 2 seconds opens the performance monitor while held.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-003
Title: Overview Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The second-row second-to-last button shall page the step overview overlay.

Details:
- This button shall stay lit white while idle.
- This button shall become brighter white while the overview overlay is active.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-004
Title: Blue Action Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The second-row last button shall act as the blue action key.

Details:
- A short press shall undo the currently selected step back to the state it had
  when selected, including notes, length, velocity, probability, and Tie.
- A hold of about 1 second shall clear the selected step.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-005
Title: Step Tools Key
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The third-row last button shall open the step tools picker for the currently
selected step.

Details:
- If no step is selected, the system shall show a status message instead of
  opening the picker.
- This button shall stay lit white while idle.
- This button shall become brighter white while the tools picker is active.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-006
Title: Step Selection
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Pressing a step pad shall select that step.

Details:
- Pressing the same selected step again shall deselect it.
- The selected step shall visually blink to show selection.
- A selected step may also be deselected with encoder press.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-007
Title: Lower Keyboard Audition
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The lower keyboard area shall remain playable in Sequencer mode for note
audition.

Details:
- With no step selected, pressing a lower keyboard note shall audition only.
- With a step selected, pressing a lower keyboard note shall audition and also
  edit the note contents of the selected step.
- With Monophonic Off, selected-step note entry shall toggle the pressed
  note in the step.
- With Monophonic On, selected-step note entry shall remove the pressed note if
  the selected step already contains that note.
- With Monophonic On, selected-step note entry shall replace the selected
  step's current notes with the pressed note when that note is not already in
  the selected step.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-008
Title: Quick Length Edit
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Turning the encoder while a step is selected shall change that step's length
through the predefined length choices.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-009
Title: Encoder Return To Menu
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Pressing the encoder while a step is selected shall deselect the step and
return to the normal Sequencer menu view.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-010
Title: Naming Save Control
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
Pressing the encoder during naming mode shall save the current file or folder
name entry.


ID: SEQ-CTRL-011
Title: Step Tools Palette
Status: Active
Area: Controls
Priority: High

Requirement:
The step tools picker shall provide the tools Len, Vel, Oct+, Oct-, Prob, Tie,
Copy, and Cancel/Finished.

Details:
- Len opens exact length entry.
- Vel opens exact velocity entry.
- Prob opens exact probability entry.
- Oct+ transposes the selected step up one current tuning cycle.
- Oct- transposes the selected step down one current tuning cycle.
- Oct+ and Oct- shall not move any selected-step note outside displayed octave
  range 0 through 9.
- If Oct+ or Oct- is blocked by that octave range, the temporary status message
  shall return to the tools picker.
- While that temporary tools status message is visible, key presses shall not
  trigger lower-keyboard note actions and shall only extend the message
  timeout.
- Tie toggles a saved per-step Tie flag for the selected step and leaves the
  picker open.
- A tied step remains editable and copyable, but transport playback treats it
  as a continuation of an earlier step rather than a new note start.
- Copy opens destination-step selection and copies notes, length, velocity, and
  probability, and Tie into the chosen destination step, then returns to the
  tools picker on that destination step.
- Cancel/Finished exits the tools picker.
- The picker's tool buttons shall be mapped to lower rows so the 4 step rows
  remain available above them.
- While the picker is open, pressing another step pad shall switch the selected
  step and keep the picker open.
- Oct+ and Oct- shall leave the tools picker open after they apply.
- If Oct+ or Oct- is chosen on an empty step, the step shall remain unchanged.
