SEQUENCER OVERVIEW SCREEN LAYOUT

The overview is paged by the second-row 9th button.

It packs in as many steps as fit on screen with no title bar, using compact
rows:

  01 C4 E4 G4
     B4 D5 F5
  02 _
  03 A2 C3 E3
  04 T
  05 G2
  06 _

Notes:

- Each press advances to the next packed page.
- Pages cycle through the full 32-step pattern.
- _ means the step is empty.
- T means the step is tied and displays no note labels of its own.
- Chord notes are displayed left to right in ascending order.
- Single-line steps do not reserve a blank second line.
- Larger chords continue onto a second indented line for the same step.
- In 12 EDO, note labels use note names like `C4`.
- In other tunings, note labels use `step.octave` labels like `7.4`.
